# Pokemon Ruby play-time counter disassembly

`axv-j-rev0-retail` is the Japanese origin reference. All 13 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish) contain one complete five-function counter cluster with the `direct` storage profile. Independent ROM evidence verifies the reset/start/stop/update/max states, 60/60/60 rollover thresholds, 999-hour clamp, save offsets, CFG boundaries and SetToMax calls without publishing ROM bytes or instruction halfwords.
