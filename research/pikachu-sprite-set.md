# Ruby Pikachu sprite set

`axv-j-rev0-retail` is the Japanese origin reference. Across all 13 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish), a unique front/back pair is selected by the `0x14A0` table delta and its normal palette by the `0xDC0` delta. LZ77 compressed, decoded, and output hashes are retained without ROM bytes.
