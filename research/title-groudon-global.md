# Global title-screen Groudon tile provenance

The Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, and Spanish revision-0 ROMs
store the same 1,945-byte compressed Groudon stream at different offsets.
Every stream decompresses to the same 8,192-byte 4bpp tile set, SHA-256
`93c1ec4ecac6e99d33dbc47fb84a2350b11a01d578066744f79d506cc48ca862`.

Language-specific extraction reports and rendered PNGs are retained so each
official release remains independently reproducible. There was no official
Korean Generation III release. The equal PNG hashes are evidence of identical
tile and palette inputs, not deduplicated or inferred outputs. No ROM bytes are
published.
