# Pokemon Ruby palette swatches

All 1 existing JASC palettes (Japanese) are rendered as deterministic RGB PNG swatches. Japanese assets remain the origin reference where that asset family has a Japanese palette; official international palettes remain separate records.

Each 16x16 cell represents one palette entry without interpolation or color correction. Evidence links the PNG to the exact palette hash and to the pre-existing ROM-derived manifest. No ROM bytes are copied into the catalog.
